LEARNED — Strategy Notes

These are the key things learned about signal trading strategy design
from building this bot.(add more to it the more you trade so you learn from your mistakes)


RSI (Relative Strength Index)
------------------------------
- RSI measures momentum, not direction. A reading below 30 means the asset
  has been sold hard and fast, not that it will definitely recover.
- The classic 30/70 thresholds work better on higher timeframes (1h, 4h, 1d).
  On 1-minute or 5-minute charts they fire too often and produce noise.
- RSI divergence (price makes a new low but RSI does not) is a stronger
  signal than a simple threshold cross, but it requires comparing two candles
  rather than just the latest value.
- RSI alone is a weak signal. It is most useful as a filter layered on top
  of a trend-following signal, not as a standalone entry trigger.


EMA Crossover
--------------
- A crossover is a lagging signal by definition — it only confirms after
  the move has already started. The shorter the periods, the more lag is
  reduced but the more false crosses appear.
- The 9/21 EMA pair works well for short-term swing setups. The 50/200 pair
  (the "golden cross" and "death cross") is better for macro trend detection.
- EMA crossovers produce the most reliable signals when the market is
  trending. In a sideways or choppy market they generate frequent false
  signals that whipsaw in both directions.
- Adding a volume confirmation check (volume above its own moving average
  at the time of the cross) dramatically reduces false signals.


MACD
-----
- MACD is a trend-following momentum indicator. It is derived from two EMAs
  so it inherits their lag.
- The histogram crossing zero is the earliest MACD signal. The MACD line
  crossing the signal line is the classic signal but is slightly later.
- MACD works best on trending assets. Like EMA crossovers, it produces
  many false signals in ranging markets.
- Normalizing the histogram by the asset's price (dividing by close) makes
  it comparable across different assets — a raw histogram value of 50 means
  something very different for BTC vs SOL.


Bollinger Bands
----------------
- Price touching the lower band is not a buy signal on its own. In a strong
  downtrend, price can "walk the band" and stay near the lower band for
  extended periods.
- Band squeeze (when upper and lower bands contract close together) signals
  low volatility and often precedes a large move in either direction. The
  direction of the breakout is not predictable from the squeeze alone.
- A close outside the band followed by a close back inside is a stronger
  mean-reversion signal than a touch alone.


Combining signals (confluence)
--------------------------------
- No single indicator is reliable enough to trade on alone. The strongest
  setups occur when multiple indicators agree at the same time — for example,
  RSI oversold AND price at the lower Bollinger Band AND MACD histogram
  turning positive.
- Requiring confluence reduces signal frequency but dramatically improves
  signal quality. Fewer trades with higher win rate is generally preferable
  to many trades with low win rate.
- Conflicting signals (e.g. RSI overbought but EMA just gave a bullish cross)
  are a reason to stay out, not a reason to pick a side.


Timeframe selection
--------------------
- Higher timeframes produce fewer but more reliable signals. A BUY signal
  on the 4-hour chart carries more weight than one on the 5-minute chart.
- Many professional strategies use multiple timeframes: confirm the trend
  on a higher timeframe (e.g. 1d or 4h) and time the entry on a lower
  timeframe (e.g. 15m or 1h).
- The bot's poll interval should match the candle timeframe. Polling every
  60 seconds while watching 1-hour candles means most cycles produce no
  new information — that is intentional and correct.


Deduplication and cooldowns
-----------------------------
- Without a cooldown, a bot will fire the same signal on every poll cycle
  for as long as the condition holds (e.g. RSI stays below 30 for 3 hours
  = 180 identical BUY alerts). A 5-minute cooldown per symbol/strategy pair
  is the minimum sensible setting.
- Cooldown length should scale with timeframe. On a 1-hour chart, a 4-6
  hour cooldown prevents re-alerting on the same setup across multiple candles.


General lessons
----------------
- Backtesting on historical data is mandatory before trusting any strategy
  with real money. A strategy that looks good on paper often fails in live
  markets due to slippage, spread, and execution lag.
- Signal strength labels (STRONG, MODERATE, WEAK) are only as meaningful
  as the logic behind them. Tying strength to how far a value exceeds a
  threshold (e.g. RSI at 18 is stronger than RSI at 28) is more useful than
  assigning it arbitrarily.
- Markets change character over time. A strategy tuned on 2021 bull market
  data will behave differently in a 2022-style bear market. Parameters should
  be reviewed periodically, not set and forgotten.
